## 2. M1 scope statement (honest deviations)

At M1 the progress was measured with `safety_stock_method: none`, which truthfully
declares the service level *out of scope* rather than silently ignoring it. The
final configuration uses `normal_approx`. Two ordering deviations are recorded:

1. D1's code fix landed at M1; its perturbation test lands at M4. Runnability
   precedence forbids blocking M1 on a Tier B test.
2. D11/D2/D20 (M2) precede D3/D7 (M3) and D4/D5 (M5) because restock attribution
   changes the inventory series that the later defects read.

### Tier B — produces defensible figures

All B1–B22 gates are green under their recorded tests. Two qualifications are
stated rather than hidden:

- `LagRegression` is a *supervised* baseline. The D1 perturbation invariant applies
  to the naive family; for lag regression the invariant is that its feature matrix
  never contains target columns (asserted in `test_baseline_no_leakage.py`).
- The safety-stock buffer uses the standard deviation of training demand as the
  variability proxy (no residual series is persisted). Switching to `none` removes
  the buffer and declares the service level out of scope.

**B23 — sign-policy gate.** `cleaning.negative_sales_policy` is validated at S0,
applied once after coercion and before attributes, counted pre-policy, and named in
`quality.csv`, `bundle.json` and the Overview panel. Evidence:
`tests/unit/test_cleaning.py -k "negative or sign or policy"` and the drill in §5.

**B24 — unit-contract gate.** Every reported quantity is in `raw_sales_units`; a
scaled read without a declared factor aborts at S0; `convert_scaled_to_raw` is the
only conversion; `codes/check_units` finds no scale literal. No source defect
produced a unit leak — B24 exists because a unit leak would leave every runnability
gate green.

**B25 — selection gate.** Candidates are fitted on train, scored on validation only,
the chosen model is refitted on train ∪ validation and the test partition is read
once per candidate through a seal guard; `bundle.selection` publishes the rule, every
validation figure, `fit_span`, `test_reads` and the outcome. Evidence:
`tests/unit/test_selection.py`, `tests/integration/test_selection_pipeline.py`.

**B26 — feature-set gate.** The matrix is closed and declared; lags and discount
lags come only from the input window; aggregates are computed from input weeks and
are invariant to every target-window value. Evidence: `tests/unit/test_feature_set.py`.

**B28 — chapter pack.** Every figure traces to a bundle field, carries a unit and its
REPs, and regeneration is byte-identical. Evidence: `tests/unit/test_chapter_pack.py`.

**B27 / B29 — not evaluated.** `data_class = sample`, so the real-data and secondary
gates are declared *not evaluated — precondition absent* in `bundle.conditional` and
in the runbook. They are never reported green.

Status: **delivered, figures provisional** — quotable only with their REP labels.

## 5. Replacement drills

**Configuration-only drill — `SEAM-SAFETY-STOCK: normal_approx -> none`.**
No file outside `src/replenish/safety.py` and its test was edited. Under
`none` the reorder point falls to lead-time demand and the assumptions block states
"service level OUT OF SCOPE (REP-10)". The full suite stays green under both values.

**Configuration-only drill — `SEAM-NEGATIVE-SALES: clip_at_zero -> retain_as_returns`.**
Executed on the 40×8 fixture with `negative_weeks=12`. `quality.negative_cells`
stays **identical at 12** under both policies; the reorder point moves
`51.8321 -> 52.4803`. On this fixture WAPE and the sell-out week did not move, which
is recorded rather than papered over: the drill proves the seam switches behaviour
while the count is invariant. Zero files outside `src/preprocess/cleaning.py`, its
test and `settings.yaml` were edited. A sample-data run reports `negative_cells = 0`,
which is **not** evidence about the real file's 3,386 cells; the factor is trusted as
declared and never re-derived from the data.

**Adapter drill — `SEAM-MODEL-BACKEND: lightgbm -> sklearn_hgb`.**
Executed in the reverse direction because the `lightgbm` wheel is not installed on
the verified runtime. The forward direction remains the documented escape hatch;
A1 is not altered and no gate is waived.

## 7. Migration note (v1.3)

`config/settings.yaml` gained a `cleaning` section and two `split` keys. The loader
requires all seventeen sections, so an older settings file aborts at S0 with
`missing configuration section` naming `cleaning` — this is a fail-fast migration,
not a silent default. Add:

```yaml
cleaning:
  negative_sales_policy: clip_at_zero
split:
  preset_files: [stfore_train.csv, stfore_test.csv]
  preset_scale_factor: null
```
